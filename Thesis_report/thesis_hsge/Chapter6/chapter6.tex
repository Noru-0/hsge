\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

This chapter (1) summarizes the products and contributions of the thesis,
(2) compares the achieved results against the objectives set in
Chapter~\ref{ch:intro}, and (3) outlines directions for future work.

\section{Summary of Contributions and Products}
\label{sec:conc-summary}

This thesis set out to make small language models viable as web agents by
attacking their binding constraint: the absence of high-quality,
task-annotated interaction data. Our answer is HSGE, a pipeline that turns
an unannotated website into training data by \emph{exploring with
structure}. Rather than wandering, the pipeline accumulates a two-level
graph of what the site affords --- verified skills, lifted into planning
operators with explicit preconditions and effects --- and then composes that
graph, by chaining operators, into verified long-horizon \emph{semantic
tasks} that no single page would suggest. Every trajectory the pipeline
collects passes automated quality gates before reaching the model, and the
resulting dataset trains the model on exactly the prompts it will see in
deployment.

The work produced five concrete artifacts:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The HSGE pipeline}: an implementation of graph-guided
  exploration, skill discovery and verification, operator induction with
  vocabulary-aware predicate canonicalization, and semantic task generation
  --- runnable end to end against both deterministic mock backends (for
  testing) and live websites through a real browser.
  \item \textbf{A curation stack and dataset}: an independent episode judge,
  a thought re-annotation pass, and a prompt-parity dataset builder, together
  with the curated fine-tuning dataset collected from self-hosted WebArena
  sites.
  \item \textbf{A fine-tuned web-agent SLM}: Qwen3.5-9B adapted with LoRA on
  the collected data, served locally with the same thinking-disabled
  configuration it was trained under.
  \item \textbf{An official benchmark evaluation}: a harness that evaluates
  base and fine-tuned models on the official WebArena task suites by reusing
  the live agent's exact prompt, with results on GitLab showing the
  fine-tuned model improving over its base from 5.56\% to 6.67\% task
  success from only 352 training examples.
  % TODO: update with the four-site results once Sections 4.4-4.6 land.
  \item \textbf{An engineering experience report}: the distilled lessons and
  best practices of Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion}, together with a
  step-by-step reproduction guide (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).
\end{enumerate}

Beyond the artifacts, the thesis argues for --- and, within its scope,
demonstrates --- a general recipe: \emph{verify before reuse, structure over
flat memory, and a two-tier task hierarchy} in which cheap page-local
proposals fuel exploration while the graph, not the page, authors the
training goals that matter.

\section{Future Directions}
\label{sec:conc-future}

The results point to several natural continuations.

\textbf{Scaling data collection.} The clearest lever identified in
Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} is data volume and breadth. The pipeline is
site-agnostic by construction, and it already includes transfer machinery
for re-grounding a discovered skill on a new website; running collection
across many sites --- and letting skills transfer between them --- is the
direct path to a larger, more diverse corpus.

\textbf{Learning from verification signals.} The pipeline's quality gates
produce more than a filtered dataset: they produce \emph{labels} ---
verified successes, judged failures, and infeasibility reports. These are
exactly the ingredients of preference optimization and reinforcement
learning, which could extract training signal from the failed trajectories
that supervised fine-tuning currently discards.

\textbf{Smaller models.} A 9B model is small by frontier standards but not
by deployment ones. Distilling the fine-tuned agent's behavior into models
in the 1--3B range would test how far the data-quality-first recipe carries
as capacity shrinks.

\textbf{Skills at inference time.} In this thesis, the skill graph shapes
\emph{training data} only; the deployed model receives no runtime hints. The
graph could instead be queried at inference time --- retrieving relevant
skills or subgoals as context --- turning the same artifact into both a data
generator and a runtime memory.

\textbf{Broader and stricter evaluation.} Beyond completing the four-site
evaluation with statistical repeats, extending to benchmark families beyond
WebArena, and to constructs finer than binary success, would sharpen the
external and construct validity discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:disc-validity}.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent
Web automation will belong to the models that operators can afford to run
and are free to improve. This thesis contributes a way to give those small
models what they currently lack --- not more parameters, but better
demonstrations, manufactured by a pipeline that explores with structure and
verifies what it teaches.
